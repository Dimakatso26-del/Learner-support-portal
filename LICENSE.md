# LICENCE Types

## Learner Licence

- Granted to registered students.
- Provides access to dashboards, tasks, progress tracking, support bookings, and gamified challenges.
- Typically free or bundled with training program enrollment.

## Assessor Licence

- Granted to trainers, assessors, and administrators.
- Includes privileges for learner monitoring, booking management, and analytics.
- May require institutional subscription or organizational approval.

---

# Licence Conditions

## Access Control

- Role-based login (learner vs assessor).
- Secure authentication with email and password.

## Usage Rights

- Learners may use the portal for personal study and support.
- Assessors may use it for monitoring and reporting only.

## Restrictions

- No redistribution of portal content outside the institution.
- No unauthorized modification of system features.

---

# Compliance & Governance

## Data Protection
- Learner personal data (ID, contact, progress) must comply with POPIA (South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act).
- Assessor accounts must safeguard learner records.
